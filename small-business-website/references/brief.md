# Client brief

Use this before building. One short round of questions, then build. Do not start coding without a brief.

## The 10 questions

1. Business name, and what it does in one sentence
2. Who is it for, and where — town, area, or service area
3. Services, with a price for each (or "from" prices, or "ask for a quote")
4. Opening hours, including closed days
5. Address or service area, and how customers get there
6. Phone / WhatsApp and email — exactly as the owner gives them
7. How do customers book today? Phone, WhatsApp, walk-in, form?
8. What is the one goal? Bookings, calls, quote requests, or visits
9. Photos: real photos of the shop, staff, and work? If none, say so
10. Reviews: real reviews the owner can share? If none, say so

## Template choice

- Several services, people visit in person: Business website
- One offer, one price, one action: Landing page
- Price depends on the job: Quote tool

If the owner is unsure, pick Business website. It covers most shops.

## Placeholders

Mark every placeholder clearly in the code and in the handover, for example `TODO: add real price for haircut`. Never fill a placeholder with an invented price, review, or address and leave it unmarked.

## Prices

Use the currency the owner uses. Keep the currency symbol in one place in the code so it can be changed in one edit. Show "from" prices only when the owner confirms the starting price. Show a range only when the owner confirms the range.
