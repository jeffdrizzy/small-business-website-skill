# Design

## Mobile first

- Design at phone width first, then widen to desktop.
- No horizontal scrolling. Tap targets at least 44px.
- Hero fits on one phone screen: name, what it does, where, one button.
- Sticky call-to-action on mobile is allowed if it does not cover the form.

## Layout

- One column on mobile, two columns on desktop where it helps.
- Services as a clean list: service name, short description, price, aligned right.
- Hours as a table: day, open–close. Mark closed days.
- Reviews as short quotes with a first name or initial only.

## Colour and type

- Two colours maximum for the brand, plus black/white/grey. Use the business's real colours if it has any.
- Body text at least 16px, line height at least 1.5, high contrast.
- One font for headings, one for body, at most. System fonts are fine for speed.

## Photos

- Real photos only. Compress them before use.
- If there are no photos, use colour blocks, large type, and spacing. Do not use stock photos that pretend to be the business.
- Every image needs alt text that says what is in the photo.

## Accessibility

- Real `button` and `a` elements, not divs that look like buttons.
- Labels on every form field.
- Focus visible when tabbing.
- Colour is never the only way to show information — also use text.
