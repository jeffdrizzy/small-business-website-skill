# Example: Barbershop — business website

Business type: barbershop. Template: Business website. Goal: bookings.

## Sections

1. Hero: "Fade & Co. — Barbershop in [town]. Sharp cuts, no waiting around." Button: Book on WhatsApp. Phone number visible.
2. Services and prices — use the owner's real prices, these are placeholders only:
   - Haircut — [CURRENCY] 25
   - Skin fade — [CURRENCY] 30
   - Beard trim — [CURRENCY] 15
   - Haircut + beard — [CURRENCY] 40
3. How it works: 1) Book a time, 2) Come in, 3) Pay after your cut.
4. Reviews: only real reviews supplied by the owner.
5. Booking: WhatsApp button + form (name, service, date, phone).
6. Hours and location: Mon–Sat table, Sunday closed, address, map link.
7. Contact footer: phone, WhatsApp, hours again.

## Copy prompts

- Hero: name the service and the area, not a slogan.
- Services: name, what is included, price. No vague "premium experience" without a price.
- Booking: "Pick a service, pick a day, we confirm by WhatsApp."

## WhatsApp message

Pre-filled: `Hi, I'd like to book a haircut on [date] at [time].`
